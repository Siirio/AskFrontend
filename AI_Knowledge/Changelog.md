2026-07-15 | **Phase 0b: the design tokens landed (D13) — `design-system/tokens.css` is the visual authority.** Also discovered that D3 chose Tailwind v4 but nobody ever installed it: no `tailwindcss` in package.json, no postcss config, `globals.css` empty. Added the deps + `postcss.config.mjs` (no new decision — D3 already made it). Authored in OKLCH so ramps are perceptually honest. The three problems the inherited direction left OPEN are closed **by measurement**: (a) **the accent** `oklch(0.575 0.161 46)` — hue 46 chosen deliberately between Amazon's yellow-orange (63) and the dusty terracotta of the AI-design cliché; chroma 0.161 is the sRGB gamut ceiling there (0.19 was out of gamut and would have been silently clipped); lightness 0.575 is the BRIGHTEST orange that still holds white text at AA (4.67:1) — one step brighter and the label fails, which is why the accent is deep: contrast chose it, not taste. Dark carries a *different* accent value at the *same* hue because one value provably cannot serve both (the light accent measures 3.84:1 on the dark surface — legal but muddy), so `--accent-foreground` is a token: white in light, ink in dark. (b) **the discount collision solved by REGISTER, not hue — "saturation is action, tint is information"** (now a lock): the accent is the only high-chroma fill and marks only actionable things; a Unique Offer is a low-chroma tint (chroma 0.032 vs 0.161) whose weight comes from bold tabular numerals. They cannot compete because they are not in the same register — and every marketplace scores discounts in red, where ASK states them as a fact. (c) **the typeface is Golos Text** (Cyrillic-native, drawn for a high-traffic public-service UI where people finish a task and leave — literally our mission). **Non-obvious find, now a lock:** ₸ the TENGE SIGN (U+20B8) lives in the `latin-ext` subset and in NO Cyrillic range — a ru/kk product that naturally loads only cyrillic+cyrillic-ext would silently fall every price back to a system font. Verified against the Google Fonts API, not assumed. Two more things the math caught: `success` was out of sRGB gamut and was clamped; `foreground-muted` sat only 0.03 lightness from the AA-floored `foreground-subtle` — three text levels where two are indistinguishable is a fake hierarchy, so muted was pushed to 0.45. **31/31 contrast pairs pass, 0 out of gamut.** Components never write `dark:` for colour (`@theme inline` resolves utilities to primitives the dark media query redefines); no theme toggle, because the vision has no such control (P9.1). **BUILD-VERIFIED, same day** (owner approved the install): `npm run build` green end to end — lint → boundary fixtures (all 5 still fail as designed) → tsc → 9 routes with the static/dynamic split intact. Driven in a real browser in both grounds: the body paints from the tokens, `--accent-foreground` genuinely flips white→ink, and ₸ + the Kazakh letters resolve from Golos rather than falling back (proven by forcing the lazy subset load — `document.fonts.check()` returns a FALSE NEGATIVE on a page that contains no ₸, because unicode-range subsets are only fetched when a glyph in range is painted; the width against a no-webfont control confirms it independently). **The two new Design Locks now have TEETH:** `e2e/design-system.spec.ts` asserts the *wiring*, not the values — body renders on the surface token, dark redefines it, the accent-foreground differs across grounds, the webfont covers ₸. A token may be retuned freely; the system cannot be silently unwired.
