# Ask

## Register

product

## Users

- Customers in Kazakhstan searching for local Items and Services through natural-language intent.
- Business owners and Staff maintaining their Business profile, branches, catalog, and conversations.
- Ask platform moderators, administrators, and super administrators operating safety, support, managed imports, accounts, and platform access.

## Product Purpose

Ask is a search-first local discovery and communication platform. It turns a customer's raw request into relevant Item or Service results and gives Businesses a direct, brand-aware path to respond and communicate.

The product succeeds when customers find a trustworthy local option quickly, Businesses can maintain accurate information without operational friction, and the Ask team can resolve risk without slowing normal publication.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, local, and trustworthy. Ask should feel like a native working product rather than a generic marketplace, template dashboard, or promotional landing page.

## Anti-references

- Marketplace grids, cheapest-first comparison, buy boxes, fake stock, fake ratings, and invented operational claims.
- Generic AI SaaS dashboards with purple gradients, decorative metrics, nested cards, giant icons, and empty decoration.
- Over-contrasty admin interfaces, glassmorphism, over-rounded panels, and dense tables without hierarchy.
- Interfaces that expose backend terminology inconsistently or invent client-side truth.

## Design Principles

1. Put the user's current task before navigation, decoration, and metrics.
2. Preserve data truth and make unknown, blocked, pending, and failed states explicit.
3. Keep standard product affordances familiar, consistent, and keyboard-accessible.
4. Use density deliberately: compact lists for scanning, focused detail panes for decisions.
5. Let semantic color communicate status and urgency; do not use it decoratively.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, complete keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion behavior, touch targets suitable for mobile, and layouts resilient to long Russian, Kazakh, and English text. Severity must never be communicated by color alone.
